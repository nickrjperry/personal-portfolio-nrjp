Selected work

Problem

  • Clinicians are bound to the EHR for patient data, and the EHR knows nothing about what happens between appointments.
  • That gap is where adherence quietly slips, symptoms escalate and treatment decisions get made on five week old information.
  • The patient-reported and biometric data existed. It just had nowhere clinical to land.

Solution

  • Dawn apps collect patient-reported and biometric data, then deliver it into the clinician workflow through Microsoft Dragon Copilot.
  • Adaptive cards surface adherence, new symptoms and trend direction the moment the chart opens. No second login, no new interface to learn.
  • Four flows behind it: Recap, Record, Signal and Sync, each tied to a real moment in the visit.

Outcome

87% HCP support

for the AI-generated pre-consultation report
  • 4.2 minutes saved per consult on chart review.
  • 3.1x more patient-reported data reaching the clinician before the visit.
  • 61% of flagged adherence drops actioned in the same appointment.

Problem

  • Hearing aids cost thousands, take weeks to fit, and carry a stigma most people will not accept.
  • So people wait. Years, usually, and many never get past the clinic door at all.
  • Meanwhile the hardware sitting in their pocket already met the spec.

Solution

  • Built a mobile hearing test validated against a machine learning model trained on hundreds of thousands of audiograms and their real-world fittings.
  • Wrote the resulting audiogram into Apple Health, which turns AirPods and compatible Beats into low-powered hearing aids.
  • Priced it as a small monthly subscription instead of a medical device purchase, on hardware people already own.

Outcome

Roughly 50x cheaper than a fitted hearing aid.

  • Acquired. Terms confidential.
  • Co-founded and led product from audiogram research through to a shipped iOS product.

Problem

  • Growth hormone deficiency means a daily injection through childhood. Miss enough of them and you lose centimetres you never get back.
  • Fear of needles is the real adherence problem, and the legacy platform treated it as a compliance issue rather than a design one.
  • The ecosystem had grown into three disconnected pieces: a patient app, a clinician portal, and two connected injection devices, one Wi-Fi and one Bluetooth.

Solution

  • Ran a heuristic evaluation across the whole ecosystem, then rebuilt it from scratch instead of patching around it.
  • Designed personalised journeys that step a child gradually through needle fear, rather than ignoring it and hoping for adherence.
  • Reconnected app, clinician portal and both devices into one loop, so paediatric endocrinologists see growth and adherence in the same place.

Outcome

93.7% median first-year adherence

  • Adherence tracked across 24 countries in the connected ecosystem this platform sits on.
  • Higher adherence correlated with real growth: change in height standard deviation score and height velocity, both p<0.001.
  • Clinicians can now see a child falling off treatment while there is still time to act.
Source: easypod connect observational study (ECOS), n=1190, 24 countries, 2010 to 2016. Endocrine Connections 7(8)

Problem

  • Independent, family-run food businesses in the Nordics have no marketing budget and no route to a new audience.
  • Most food content online is paid placement dressed up as a recommendation.
  • People can tell, and once they can tell they stop trusting any of it.

Solution

  • Built a review brand on one rule: only places worth going back to, and nothing paid.
  • Shot, edited and published everything myself, focused on the family behind the counter rather than the plate.
  • Kept the format short and repeatable so the audience knew exactly what they were getting.

Outcome

5M+ views. 30k followers. 300k likes.

  • Videos regularly past a million views, from a standing start with no budget.
  • A community that turned up for small businesses instead of for the algorithm.

Problem

  • Independent cafes have healthy margins per drink but unpredictable volume.
  • Quiet weeks and slow summers turn a good business into a stressful one.
  • Nobody was solving revenue stability for them. The only tool on offer was discounting, which makes it worse.

Solution

  • Roughly $50 a month for one beverage a day at any partner cafe.
  • Passed around 80% of subscription revenue straight back to the cafes as recurring monthly income.
  • Onboarded independently owned cafes only, and built the brand around exactly that.

Outcome

12 cafes. 100+ paying subscribers.

  • Turned unpredictable footfall into monthly recurring revenue for owners.
  • Customer acquisition close to zero. Growth came through the cafes themselves.
